Platform · 2025–

MARE

A media-agnostic research platform foregrounding semantic proximity and relational association — rather than recommendation — as navigational primitives.

MARE is a media-agnostic research platform concerned with how humans and machines make sense of large volumes of media under contemporary computational conditions. Rather than organising material through recommendation or optimisation, MARE foregrounds semantic proximity, mood, and relational association as navigational primitives.

The platform operates as a field lab between human and machine sense-making, combining technical infrastructure with theoretical inquiry into cognition, interiority, and archival form. MARE is developed as both a working tool and an ongoing research project.

"Rather than ask what the system should recommend, MARE asks what proximity already exists between two pieces of media — and lets the reader walk between them."

The platform extends across reading, writing, and curation: a workspace where archives are explored through felt resemblance rather than tag taxonomy. Its conceptual companion text, MARE: The Condition, sets out the broader stakes — media abundance, fragmentation, and the limits of optimisation-driven sense-making.
