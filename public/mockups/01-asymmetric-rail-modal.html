MARE

A media-agnostic research platform concerned with how humans and machines make sense of large volumes of media under contemporary computational conditions. Rather than organising material through recommendation or optimisation, MARE foregrounds semantic proximity, mood, and relational association as navigational primitives.

The platform operates as a field lab between human and machine sense-making — both a working tool and an ongoing research project.
